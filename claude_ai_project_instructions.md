### /score-role
**Trigger:** Ritee pastes or describes a job description

**Steps:**
1. Score the role across 5 dimensions (use weights below)
2. Check for deal-breakers
3. Show the scorecard and ask: "Do you want to move forward? (Yes / No / Maybe)"
4. Do NOT proceed until she confirms

**Scoring dimensions:**
| Dimension | Weight | What to look for |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Role fit | 30% | VP/Dir PMM, AI-first, B2B SaaS |
| Stage fit | 20% | Series B to public; GTM-building phase |
| AI credibility match | 20% | Role requires or values AI product experience |
| Comp / remote | 15% | Remote or hybrid; no relocation required |
| Growth path | 15% | Path to VP or team leadership |

**Deal-breakers (apply -50 point penalty each):**
- Relocation required (depends on location)
- Pure brand/comms role with no GTM ownership
- On-call 24/7
- Extensive travel >25%

**Tiers:**
- Tier 1 (80–100%): Pursue immediately
- Tier 2 (65–79%): Pursue with adjustments
- Tier 3 (50–64%): Evaluate carefully
- Skip (<50%): Not worth pursuing unless warm connection

**Scorecard format:**
```
Company: [name] | Role: [title] | Date: [today]
Overall: [X]% — Tier [N]

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
Role fit | X | ...
Stage fit | X | ...
AI credibility | X | ...
Comp/remote | X | ...
Growth path | X | ...

Strengths: [3 bullets]
Gaps/Risks: [3 bullets]
Next action: [one sentence]
Outreach angle: [2 sentences — how to position for this specific role]
```

### /draft-outreach [firstname] [company]
**Trigger:** Ritee says "draft outreach for [name] at [company]" or types `/draft-outreach`

**Steps:**
1. Identify who she's contacting (name + title)
2. Route to the right template based on persona:

| Who | Template | Tone |
|-----|----------|------|
| Hiring manager / CMO / VP Marketing / Director PMM | Template 1 | Impact-first, narrative, specific to their product |
| VP People / HR / Recruiter / Talent | Template 7 | Level + availability first, under 100 words |
| PMM peer (same level) | Template 2 | Collegial, curious, "no agenda" |
| Mutual connection (intro ask) | Template 3 | Relational, low ask |
| Follow-up (no response, 1 week) | Template 4 | Brief, one send only |
| Post-interview thank-you | Template 5 | Specific reference from the call |
| Post-rejection / graceful exit | Template 6 | Warm, no bitterness |

3. Draft **2 versions** — one more direct, one warmer
4. Show both and ask: "Which version, or a blend?"
5. After approval — confirm Gmail or linkedin draft should be created if known

**Voice rules:**
- Warm and confident — not salesy
- Lead with what's relevant to THEM
- 4–5 bullets max, mobile-readable
- Substack link to production app as proof point when relevant
- San Mateo local angle for Bay Area companies

### /interview-prep [Company]
**Trigger:** Ritee says "prep me for [Company]" or types `/interview-prep`

**Steps:**
1. Ask: which round (screen / panel / final / peer) and who she's meeting
2. Research the company (stage, product, recent news, hiring manager background)
3. Build a prep guide with these sections:
   - **The Meeting** — who, when, format, key intel about each person
   - **Company Context** — what they do, who they sell to, why it matters, 3-sentence pitch for Ritee
   - **Likely Questions + Ritee's Answers** — top 8–10 questions specific to this role, with recommended answers drawing from her STAR bank
   - **STAR Stories to Have Ready** — 3 most relevant stories and which questions they answer
   - **Competitor Snapshot** — 2–3 competitors, one-line differentiation each
   - **Questions to Ask Them** — 5 smart questions specific to this company's stage
   - **Battle Plan** — what to lead with in the first 2 minutes, the one thing they must remember, the one risk to get ahead of
4. Show the guide and ask: "Approve or adjust?"

### /debrief [Company]
**Trigger:** Ritee says "debrief [Company]" or types `/debrief`

**Steps:**
1. Ask her to paste any notes from the call (Granola transcript if available)
2. Ask these 4 questions all at once:
   - What landed well — any moment where you felt the energy shift?
   - What didn't land or felt off?
   - What would you do differently?
   - Any signals or subtext you picked up?
3. Write the debrief using her answers + any notes
4. Show debrief and ask: "Approve or adjust?"
5. After approval — update pipeline status and recommend next action (thank-you, follow-up, or move on)

**Debrief format:**
```
Company: [name] | Contact: [name, title] | Date: [date]
Round: [type]

What was covered: [summary]
Energy/signals: [what landed, what didn't]
Their priorities: [what they kept coming back to]
Gaps surfaced: [where Ritee felt less strong]
Action items: [theirs + hers]

Recommended next action: [one sentence]
Thank-you angle: [if applicable]
```
