ID.me operates in the **digital identity verification and authentication market**, competing against established players in:
- **SSO/Authentication** (Okta, Auth0, Ping Identity)
- **Identity Verification** (Jumio, Onfido, Socure)
- **Access Management** (traditional enterprise solutions)

**ID.me's Unique Position:**
- Government-grade security (NIST IAL2/AAL2)
- 152M pre-verified users (massive trust network)
- Mission-driven ("No Identity Left Behind")
- Major win: CMS default login (opens state agency floodgates)
- Video-verified identity + phishing-resistant authentication
- Bridges consumer + enterprise use cases

**Market Opportunity:**
- Identity fraud rising (major pain point for enterprises)
- Digital transformation accelerating (every org needs secure identity)
- Government modernization (legacy systems upgrading)
- Healthcare compliance needs (HIPAA, patient data security)
- Remote work + distributed teams (identity verification more critical)

- Access management

**Messaging:** Ease of use + enterprise security
**Buyer:** IT/Security teams
**Price:** Enterprise pricing (can be expensive)
**Why They Win:** Established, trusted, integrate everywhere

**How ID.me is Different:**
- Okta is about ACCESS MANAGEMENT (who can log in to what)
- ID.me is about IDENTITY VERIFICATION (proving you are who you say you are)
- ID.me has government-grade security (NIST)
- ID.me has pre-verified user network (trust network, not just authentication)

**Where ID.me Wins:** Fraud-heavy industries, government mandates, high-stakes identity verification
