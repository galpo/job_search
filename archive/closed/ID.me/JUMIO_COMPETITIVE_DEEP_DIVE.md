# JUMIO DEEP-DIVE: Business Model, Why ID.me Wins
## What You Need to Crush This Competitive Conversation

---

## WHAT IS JUMIO? (The Quick Version)

**Jumio = Identity verification specialist**

- **What they do:** Document verification (ID scanning) + liveness detection + KYC/AML compliance
- **Who buys:** Financial services, fintechs, payments companies
- **Business model:** Per-verification pricing (you pay per identity check)
- **Market position:** "Reduce identity fraud and financial crime"

**Key insight:** Jumio is verification-only. They don't do authentication or ongoing fraud prevention.

---

## JUMIO'S BUSINESS MODEL (How They Make Money)

### **Per-Verification Pricing**
- Customer: "I need to verify 10,000 users this month"
- Jumio: "That's 10,000 verifications × $X per verification = $Y"
- Result: Revenue scales with transaction volume, not with customer value

### **Why this model?**
- ✅ Easy to understand (simple transaction pricing)
- ✅ Works for fintech (fast-moving, high transaction volume)
- ✅ Can charge premium per verification (compliance is expensive)

### **The problem with this model:**
- ❌ Customer pays for EVERY verification (even repeat users)
- ❌ No incentive to reduce verification costs
- ❌ Doesn't solve re-verification problem

## JUMIO'S STRENGTHS (Why They Win Some Deals)

### 1. **Compliance Credibility**
- Strong in financial services (the hardest vertical)
- Trusted for KYC/AML (know-your-customer / anti-money-laundering)
- Deep expertise in regulatory requirements

### 2. **Document Verification Excellence**
- AI-powered document scanning (ID, passport, etc.)
- Highly accurate (catches fakes, forgeries)
- Liveness detection (proves you're alive, not a photo)
- **Who cares:** Financial institutions, high-fraud verticals

### 3. **Global Reach**
- Supports 200+ document types
- Works across geographies
- **Who cares:** Companies operating internationally

### 4. **Established Relationships**
- Been in market since 2010
- Deep relationships in fintech/payments
- **Who cares:** Existing fintech customers

## JUMIO'S WEAKNESSES (Where ID.me Crushes Them)

### 1. **Re-verification Problem**
**The issue:** Jumio verifies you today, but tomorrow when you log in, you verify again.

**Why?** Jumio doesn't authenticate. They just verify once and hand off.

**ID.me advantage:**
- Verify once → authenticate everywhere
- Pre-verified network means no re-verification needed
- Lower cost per user (one verification, many authentications)

**Jumio's cost model:** 10,000 users × 10 logins = 100,000 verifications = 100,000 charges
**ID.me's cost model:** 10,000 users × 1 verification = cost stays flat as usage grows

### 2. **No Government Credential**
**Jumio:** "We're good at compliance"
**ID.me:** "We're NIST IAL2/AAL2 certified and used by 20 federal agencies"

**Why it matters:**
- Government buyers (SLED, federal agencies) need government-blessed solutions
- Jumio has zero government business
- ID.me has a built-in government moat

**Example:** When a state CIO is evaluating, Jumio isn't even considered. ID.me is.

### 3. **No Pre-verified Network**
**Jumio:** "We verify people for you"
**ID.me:** "152M people are already verified. You tap that network immediately."

**Why it matters:**
- ID.me customers get instant value from pre-verified users
- Jumio requires every customer to verify their own users
- ID.me has network effect (more users = more valuable)

### 4. **No Authentication Layer**
**Jumio:** "We verify. Then you integrate with something else for ongoing authentication."
**ID.me:** "We verify AND authenticate in one platform."

**Why it matters:**
- More integrations = more complexity for customer
- One platform = simpler, cheaper, more secure
- ID.me = unified security model

### 5. **Fintech-Focused, Not Enterprise-Focused**
**Jumio's market:** Fintechs, payments, crypto
**ID.me's market:** SLED, Healthcare, Workforce, Federal, Communities

**Why it matters:**
- Jumio's go-to-market, messaging, product = fintech-optimized
- Enterprise buyers (especially regulated enterprise) = different psychology
- ID.me's product = built for enterprise + government

## HOW TO POSITION AGAINST JUMIO (The Sales Narrative)

### **When a prospect is evaluating Jumio:**

**Your frame:**
"Jumio is excellent at identity verification. They're the standard in fintech. But they solve for one problem — proving you are who you say you are once. We solve for three problems:

1. **Verification:** We do that (as well as Jumio)
2. **Authentication:** Ongoing, phishing-resistant (Jumio doesn't do this)
3. **Fraud Prevention:** Real-time, preventing re-verification costs (Jumio doesn't do this)

The business impact: You verify a customer once with us. They log in 100 times. You pay once. With Jumio, every time they log in, you either re-verify (costly) or integrate another auth tool (complex).

**Plus:** We have 152M pre-verified users. If your customer is already ID.me-verified (maybe through government benefits, or another company using us), they're instant frictionless for you."

### **When they ask "Isn't Jumio cheaper?"**

**Your response:**
"Jumio's price per verification is low. But the hidden cost is re-verification. You pay per transaction. We charge per platform. Here's the math:

- 100,000 users, each logs in 10 times/month = 1M verification events
- Jumio: 1M events × $0.50 = $500K/year
- ID.me: Flat platform fee for 100K users = $150K/year

You only save money with Jumio if each user logs in less than once a month."
